Executive Summary
The Project Scope

EyE PunE proposes a full turnkey Shopify redesign for E-Strip — building a custom, high-converting storefront featuring 17 homepage sections, an 11-section PDP, a 3-tier Bundle & Save page, supporting pages, a custom Ajax cart, email automation, on-page SEO setup, and complete store configuration. Once E-Strip approves the proposal and grants Shopify collaborator access, our team will execute the complete build, configuration, testing, and deployment to go live in 7 days.

Deployment Commitment

This is a focused, 7-day turnkey sprint — custom theme development, Shopify store setup, payment/shipping configuration, QA, and domain launch upon Milestone 1 signing.

Terms & Conditions
01 — Payment Terms

Each milestone payment of ₹15,000 is due as per the schedule. Milestone 1 is due on signing. Subsequent invoices are raised upon approval of the prior milestone. The full project investment is ₹60,000.

Payments not received within 5 business days of invoice will result in a project pause. A 5% late fee applies from day 6, with 2% per week thereafter.

02 — Revisions & Scope

Each milestone includes 2 rounds of revisions. Additional revisions are billed at ₹2,000 per revision. Revisions must be consolidated — one brief per round.

Scope changes (new sections, new pages, or feature additions) after milestone sign-off are treated as separate engagements and quoted accordingly.

03 — Intellectual Property

All code, design, and Liquid templates become the property of E-Strip upon full final payment. EyE PunE retains the right to showcase the project in its portfolio.

Any third-party assets (fonts, icons, stock images) used remain subject to their respective licences, which the client is responsible for maintaining post-handover.

04 — Cancellation

Work completed up to the point of cancellation is billable in full. If the client cancels mid-milestone, the full milestone amount for that phase is due.

EyE PunE may terminate the engagement with 7 days written notice if the client is unresponsive for more than 10 business days or if payments remain overdue beyond 14 days.

05 — No Monthly Maintenance

This is a custom-built theme with no ongoing monthly maintenance fees. A 30-day support window is included post-launch for bug fixes directly related to delivered code. This does not cover new feature requests, content changes, or third-party app conflicts.

Optional retainer agreements for updates are available and quoted separately only if requested.

06 — Governing Law

This agreement is governed by the laws of India. Disputes shall be resolved through good-faith negotiation, and if unresolved, through arbitration in Pune, Maharashtra under the Arbitration and Conciliation Act, 1996.

Signing this proposal constitutes agreement to all terms. A formal SLA will be issued separately prior to commencement.

This proposal covers development services only. Shopify subscription fees, paid theme licences, and third-party app costs are not included and are the client's responsibility. We recommend Shopify Basic (₹1,994/month) as a starting plan.
